Before anything else

This coin may be worth nothing. The overwhelming majority of new cryptocurrencies fail, and there is no reason to assume this one is different. Nobody is guaranteed to buy it from you. There is no price on this page and there never will be. Treat anything you acquire as money you can afford to lose entirely.

What it is

A fork of Bitcoin Core v28.1 with four deliberate departures: a hard ceiling of 22,000,000 coins, a two-minute block target, RandomX proof of work so that ordinary CPUs stay competitive against purpose-built hardware, and difficulty retargeting on every block so that a rented hash-rate spike cannot freeze the chain.

Roughly 250,000 lines of peer-to-peer networking, script interpretation and wallet code are inherited from a codebase that has been adversarially reviewed for fifteen years. Retyping that would not have made WAM more original — it would have made it less safe. About 3,000 lines are genuinely ours, and every one of them is public.

None of the founder reserve is spendable at launch. All 2,000,000 are locked in the genesis block itself by OP_CHECKLOCKTIMEVERIFY, releasing 20% a year from September 2027 to September 2031. The 5% treasury fee is a consensus rule that stops permanently at block 400,000, after which miners receive the entire subsidy. Neither is a promise; both are enforced by every node, and you can read the lock out of block 0 yourself.

What the founder does not do

The founder keeps the founder reserve and grants himself nothing above it. He does not sell WAM and does not support its price — because a founder who accumulates beyond his own share corrupts the distribution, and a coin concentrated in one hand is not distributed however its schedule reads.

If he ever needs it for something, he buys it the way you would: from a miner or a venue, at whatever it is worth that day.

This is a restriction he placed on himself for the fairness of the distribution. It is not a promise to you, and it puts a floor under nothing.

Do not take our word for it

Every claim above has a command that proves or disproves it. These read the shipped binary, not this page:

Mining

WAM is mined by ordinary computers. There is no pre-sale, no allocation to buy into, and no way to acquire it at launch except by mining it or by trading with someone who did.

Mainnet opens 15 September 2026. Until then the testnet is running, and its coins are worth nothing by design.

The founder mines too — same software, same public pool, same difficulty as anyone else, and the addresses he mines to will be published here. A new chain with few miners is a chain that is cheap to attack, so he is the floor under its security until others arrive. His share of the hash rate will be large at launch and is meant to fall; it will be published so you can watch it fall rather than take his word for it.

Trading

WAM is not listed anywhere, and there is no price. It cannot be bought.

Enquiries have been sent to six decentralised venues — Komodo Wallet, BasicSwap DEX, Bisq, Maya Protocol, Haveno and Block DX — asking what they require in order to list a chain like this. None of them has agreed to anything. They are requests for requirements, sent before launch because reviews at venues of this kind take weeks, and this line will say something different only when one of them says yes.

WAM is its own layer 1 rather than a token on someone else's chain, so it settles by atomic swap or a peer-to-peer order book. It cannot appear on an automated market maker such as Uniswap without a bridge and a wrapped representation, which is the same reason Monero does not.

ما هي

تفريعة من Bitcoin Core v28.1 بأربعة اختلافات مقصودة: سقف نهائي قدره 22,000,000 وحدة، وبلوك كل دقيقتين، وإثبات عمل RandomX ليبقى المعالج العادي منافساً أمام العتاد المصنوع خصيصاً للتعدين، وإعادة ضبط للصعوبة عند كل بلوك حتى لا تستطيع قوة تعدين مستأجرة أن تُجمّد السلسلة.

نحو 250,000 سطر من شبكة الند للند وتفسير السكربت وإدارة المحفظة موروثة من قاعدة كود خضعت لمراجعة عدائية طوال خمسة عشر عاماً. إعادة كتابتها ما كانت لتجعل WAM أكثر أصالة — كانت لتجعلها أقل أماناً. نحو 3,000 سطر هي ملكنا فعلاً، وكلها منشورة.
