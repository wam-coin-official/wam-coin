What it is

A fork of Bitcoin Core v28.1 with four deliberate departures: a hard ceiling of 22,000,000 coins, a two-minute block target, RandomX proof of work so that ordinary CPUs stay competitive against purpose-built hardware, and difficulty retargeting on every block so that a rented hash-rate spike cannot freeze the chain.

Roughly 250,000 lines of peer-to-peer networking, script interpretation and wallet code are inherited from a codebase that has been adversarially reviewed for fifteen years. Retyping that would not have made WAM more original — it would have made it less safe. About 3,000 lines are genuinely ours, and every one of them is public.

The founder reserve is locked in the genesis block itself by OP_CHECKLOCKTIMEVERIFY, releasing 20% a year to September 2030. The 5% treasury fee is a consensus rule that stops permanently at block 400,000, after which miners receive the entire subsidy. Neither is a promise; both are enforced by every node.

Mining

WAM is mined by ordinary computers. There is no pre-sale, no allocation to buy into, and no way to acquire it at launch except by mining it or by trading with someone who did.

Mainnet opens 15 September 2026. Until then the testnet is running, and its coins are worth nothing by design.
